Why Supplier Price Tracking Matters for Independent Restaurants

Independent restaurants typically buy from 3-5 different suppliers — a main distributor like Sysco or US Foods, plus local bread vendors, egg farmers, meat suppliers, and produce companies. Every one of those suppliers adjusts prices regularly, and most restaurant owners have no system to catch those changes.

KitchenIQ solves this by scanning every invoice from every supplier and automatically tracking price changes over time. When your egg cost jumps 15% or your beef goes up $4 per case, you know immediately — not at the end of the month when you're already losing money.

The Problem With Supplier-Specific Tools

Some distributors offer their own cost tracking tools — but these only work for purchases made through that distributor. If you buy bread from a local bakery, eggs from a local farm, or meat from a regional supplier, those prices are completely invisible to distributor-specific tools.

KitchenIQ works with every invoice from every supplier. Scan them all and see your complete cost picture in one place.

How KitchenIQ Tracks Supplier Prices

Every time a new invoice comes in, you take a photo with your phone. KitchenIQ's AI reads every line item — ingredient name, price, case size, and unit — and compares it to your previous invoice from the same supplier. Any price change triggers an instant alert showing exactly what changed, by how much, and which menu items are affected.
