Why Manual Invoice Entry Doesn't Work

Most independent restaurant owners handle supplier invoices one of two ways — they file them away and forget about them, or they manually type prices into a spreadsheet that quickly goes stale. Neither approach catches price changes in time to do anything about them.

KitchenIQ replaces manual invoice entry entirely. Scan the invoice the day it arrives, and your entire ingredient database updates automatically with the latest prices.

Works With Every Supplier's Invoice Format

Every distributor formats their invoices differently. Sysco invoices look completely different from US Foods invoices, which look different from a local bakery's handwritten invoice. KitchenIQ's AI is trained to read every format — printed, handwritten, photographed, or scanned.

What KitchenIQ Extracts From Every Invoice

For each line item on your invoice, KitchenIQ automatically extracts the ingredient name in a standardized format, the total case price, the case size and unit, the supplier name, and the invoice date. This data is then used to calculate your per-unit cost for every ingredient and track how prices change over time.
