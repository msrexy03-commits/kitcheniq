Why Most Restaurant Owners Get Margins Wrong

Most independent restaurant owners estimate food cost by feel — a rough percentage they remember from when they set menu prices years ago. The problem is supplier prices change constantly, and that number is almost always wrong by the time you actually check it.

KitchenIQ calculates your actual food cost percentage on every dish using your most recent supplier prices. When your egg cost goes up 20% in February, your Bacon and Eggs plate margin drops automatically and you see it immediately.

How Restaurant Margin Calculation Works in KitchenIQ

You build a recipe for each menu item — selecting the ingredients you use and the quantities per plate. KitchenIQ then looks up your current cost for each ingredient based on your most recent supplier invoice and calculates the total ingredient cost per serving. Divide that by your menu price and you get your food cost percentage. Subtract from 100% and you have your margin.

The difference with KitchenIQ is that this calculation updates automatically every time a supplier price changes. You don't recalculate manually — it's always current.

What Is a Good Restaurant Profit Margin?

A healthy food cost percentage for independent restaurants is typically 28-35% of the menu price, which translates to a food margin of 65-72%. Fine dining restaurants often target lower food cost percentages around 25-30%. Diners and casual restaurants typically run 30-38% food cost. KitchenIQ's default target is 65% margin which you can adjust to match your specific goals.
